## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AI Agents 101?
It's an interactive field guide to how AI agents and large language models actually work — a set of hands-on demos plus a linked notes vault. The goal: learn from the source, not the hype.

### Do I need an internet connection?
No. The App is fully offline. Every demo and note ships inside the App and runs on your device.

### Do I need Apple Intelligence or a newer iPhone?
No. The App runs on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. On supported devices, one demo (the Writer ↔ Reviewer loop) can optionally use Apple's on-device models; everywhere else it runs in scripted "mock" mode. No special hardware is required.

### What do the REAL and MOCK badges mean?
Every demo is labeled so you always know what's running:
- **REAL** — genuine logic is executing on your device (for example, a real syllable-counting tool or real cosine-similarity math).
- **MOCK** — the demo is a scripted illustration of the concept, not a live model.

### Is my data collected?
No. The App collects nothing — no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no network requests. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy) for details.

### How do tips work?
The App is free and nothing is gated. If you'd like to support development, the optional tip jar uses Apple's In-App Purchase system. Tips unlock nothing — they're just a thank-you.

### How do I restore a tip?
Tips are consumable, so there's nothing to restore — but if you were charged in error, contact Apple Support or email us and we'll help.

### How do I delete my data?
There's nothing to delete on any server (the App stores nothing remotely). To remove the App and everything bundled with it, simply delete the App from your device.

### Where is the notes vault?
Open the **Notes Vault** from the home screen to browse the 33 linked concept notes inside the App. A companion field-guide PDF and the source notes are also available separately.
