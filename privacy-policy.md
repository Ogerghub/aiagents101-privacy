# Privacy Policy — AI Agents 101

**Last updated:** July 19, 2026

AI Agents 101 ("the App") is developed by Cengiz Oger. This privacy policy explains what data the App collects and how it is handled.

## Summary

**AI Agents 101 collects no data whatsoever.** The App is fully offline. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no user accounts, and makes no network requests. Nothing you do in the App ever leaves your device.

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- **No accounts** — there is no sign-in, registration, or profile.
- **No analytics or tracking** — the App contains no analytics SDKs, no tracking identifiers, and no third-party trackers.
- **No advertising** — there are no ads and no ad networks.
- **No network requests** — the App does not connect to the internet. All content (interactive demos and the bundled notes vault) ships inside the App and runs entirely on your device.

## On-Device Content

All of the App's content — the concept demos and the linked notes vault — is bundled with the App and read locally. Any interaction you have with a demo happens on your device and is not recorded or transmitted.

## Apple Intelligence (optional, on-device only)

On supported devices, one demo (the Writer ↔ Reviewer loop) can use Apple's on-device **Foundation Models**. This processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's frameworks; no data is sent to the developer or to any third party. On other devices, the demo runs in scripted "mock" mode with no model at all.

## Tips / In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional "tip jar" using Apple's In-App Purchase system. If you choose to leave a tip, the transaction is processed entirely by **Apple**. The developer never receives your payment details or any personal information — only Apple's anonymous confirmation that a purchase occurred. Tips are optional and unlock nothing; all features are free.

## Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.
